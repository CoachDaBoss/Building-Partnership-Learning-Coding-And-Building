# Legal / Product Boundaries

The platform should educate users while avoiding claims that it is acting as their lawyer, tax preparer, accountant, manager or royalty collection society unless IT HIT DIFFERENT LLC later enters those businesses with appropriate professional and contractual infrastructure.

## Copyright
A release record separates composition ownership from sound-recording/master ownership. Users attest rights before publishing. Disputes should preserve evidence, freeze or limit access when required by policy, and route through a counsel-reviewed notice/counter-notice process.

## Business education
DBA/assumed-name, entity, tax and licensing requirements vary by jurisdiction. Give users official-resource links and checklists, then recommend qualified professionals for individualized decisions.

## Management / legal referrals
A directory can help users understand how to vet entertainment lawyers, managers, agents and accountants. Avoid representing unverified providers as endorsed professionals.

## Privacy
Publish a counsel-reviewed privacy policy describing identity/account data, music files, chats, live-session metadata, security logs, analytics, retention, exports/deletion, subprocessors, minors, cookies, regional rights and incident response.
